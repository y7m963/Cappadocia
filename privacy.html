Privacy Policy

This travel blog is a student project. If you use the contact form, we only process the data you enter there so we can read and, if needed, reply to your message.

What we collect

Name, email address, and the text of your message. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Why we use it

The details are used only to handle your enquiry, for example a question about a destination or a possible collaboration.

How long we keep it

Messages are kept only as long as needed to answer you, and are then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we have stored, request a correction, or ask us to delete it. Use the contact form on the homepage for that.
